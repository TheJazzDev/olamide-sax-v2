The Journey

A career, composed.

Read from the first drum to the newest stage — each milestone a movement, spaced along the breath. The line carries the story from the rhythm of home to the civic halls of the UK.

Milestones along the line

  1. 2010

    The first rhythm

    It begins in Yoruba percussion — the cord/omele, the djembe, the omele bàtá and the talking drum. Learning to speak in rhythm before melody.

  2. Early years

    Toward many voices

    The percussionist develops into a multi-instrumentalist — saxophone, vocals and keyboard join the pulse, and Afro-fusion takes shape.

  3. A banner of his own

    A home for the sound

    Olamide establishes a banner of his own for the Afro-fusion work — later growing into Lammy Wonder Music Entertainment, the umbrella for the performances and projects to come.

  4. Oct 2025

    Doncaster, in season

    Nigeria in Doncaster Cultural Day, then a Black History Month set for the BME United Doncaster event — vocals, piano and saxophone for community and civic leaders.

    Doncaster · Black History Month

  5. Mar 2026

    “Mama”

    Written, composed and performed for Stand & Be Counted Theatre’s Soapbox Spotlight in Sheffield — an original Afro-fusion work for vocals and saxophone.

    SBC Soapbox Spotlight · Sheffield

  6. Apr 2026

    ÀTÙPÀ (The Spotlight)

    Opening saxophone for the stage production ÀTÙPÀ at CAST, Doncaster — performing in the role of Adigún for the Black Pride Festival.

    CAST · Black Pride Festival · As Adigún

  7. Apr 2026

    Beyond the Scroll

    A featured saxophonist for ArtBomb UK in Doncaster — a live collaboration with The Skintones UK and an outdoor public performance activation.

    ArtBomb UK · With The Skintones UK

  8. Jun 2026

    World Record Football Scarf Procession

    Leading live saxophone with Stand & Be Counted Theatre for the Guinness World Record football scarf procession (186 metres) in Sheffield — a public celebration of belonging, migration and cultural identity.

    Stand & Be Counted Theatre · Sheffield

  9. Jun 2026

    Manchester Africa Day

    A featured performing artist on vocals and saxophone at the Manchester Africa Day Cultural Festival.

    Manchester · Vocals & saxophone

  10. Jul 2026

    Sharrow Festival

    A featured member of Sound Café’s Afrobeat ensemble on the community stage — high-energy live sets and a shared celebration of rhythm, dance and cultural exchange.

    Sheffield · Sound Café Afrobeat ensemble

  11. 2026

    Eko Heritage Awards

    Named Best Instrumentalist of the Year at the Eko Heritage Awards — recognition for a decade of Afro-fusion performance.

    Winner · Best Instrumentalist of the Year

— and the next movement is still being written.
