The Journey

A career, composed.

Read from the first drum to the newest stage — each milestone a movement, spaced along the breath. The line carries the story from the rhythm of home to the civic halls of the UK.

♪ Milestones along the line

  1. 2010

    The first rhythm

    It begins in Yoruba percussion — the cord/omele, the djembe, the omele bàtá and the talking drum. Learning to speak in rhythm before melody.

  2. Early years

    Toward many voices

    The percussionist develops into a multi-instrumentalist — saxophone, vocals and keyboard join the pulse, and Afro-fusion takes shape.

  3. A banner of his own

    A home for the sound

    Olamide establishes a banner of his own for the Afro-fusion work — later growing into Lammy Wonder Music Entertainment, the umbrella for the performances and projects to come.

  4. On stage

    ÀTÙPÀ at CAST

    Opening saxophone for the stage production ÀTÙPÀ at CAST — performing in the role of Adigun.

    CAST · As Adigun

  5. Collaboration

    ArtBomb

    A collaborative performance with The Skintone UK — the sound meeting other artists on a shared stage.

    With The Skintone UK

  6. Recent · procession

    World Record Football Scarf Procession

    Leading live saxophone with Stand & Be Counted Theatre during the record-breaking public procession — thousands brought together through music, celebration and participation.

    Stand & Be Counted Theatre

  7. Recent · festival

    Sharrow Festival

    A featured member of Sound Café’s Afrobeat ensemble — high-energy live sets and a shared celebration of rhythm, dance and cultural exchange.

    Sound Café Afrobeat ensemble

  8. Recent · multidisciplinary

    Eco Art Fashion Show

    Live Afro-fusion saxophone woven through sustainable fashion, visual arts and audience interaction.

    Live saxophone · participatory

  9. Recent · civic stages

    Before the Mayor of Doncaster

    Black History Month, Doncaster — an Afro-fusion set performed for the Mayor and council leaders.

    Doncaster · Black History Month

  10. Recent · community

    BME United Doncaster

    A performance for the Black and Minority Ethnic United Doncaster event — saxophone, voice and the rhythm of home for community and civic leaders.

  11. Recent · Sheffield

    Spotlight, Sheffield

    SBC Spotlight in Sheffield — a stirring performance before the Lord Mayor.

    Sheffield · Before the Lord Mayor

  12. Ongoing

    darts × Maya Productions

    A traineeship with darts in partnership with Maya Productions — craft sharpened alongside the stage.

    Traineeship

  13. 2026

    Eko Heritage Awards

    Named Best Instrumentalist of the Year at the Eko Heritage Awards — recognition for a decade of Afro-fusion performance.

    Winner · Best Instrumentalist of the Year

  14. 2026

    Open Mic, Utopia Theatre

    1 March 2026 — an intimate improvisational set at Utopia Theatre, moving between saxophone, keys and vocals.

    1 Mar 2026 · Utopia Theatre

— and the next movement is still being written.
