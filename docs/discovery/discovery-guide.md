# Olamide Sax — Getting Found: Wikipedia, Google & Discovery

A practical action list. Two parts:
- **Part A — Do now** (achievable today, helps Google immediately).
- **Part B — The road to Wikipedia** (the things HE needs to do to become
  eligible; this is a campaign over months, not a one-off task).

The honest headline: **Wikipedia is earned, not requested.** You can't pay for a
page or write your own — Wikipedia only accepts a page once *other people*
(journalists, established outlets) have written enough substantial, independent
articles **about him**. So most of the work below is really about **earning more
press coverage**. That press is the raw material a Wikipedia page is built from.

## PART B — THE ROAD TO WIKIPEDIA

### The one rule that decides everything

Wikipedia's bar (WP:MUSICBIO / general notability): **significant coverage in
multiple, independent, reliable published sources.**

Decode that:
- **Significant** = a whole article (or a substantial section) *about him* — not
  a one-line mention, not a listing, not an event write-up that names him in
  passing.
- **Independent** = written by someone with no connection to him. His own site,
  his socials, press releases, and the orgs he works with (darts, ArtBomb, SBC)
  **do not count**.
- **Reliable** = established outlets with editorial standards (national
  newspapers, magazines, broadcasters).
- **Multiple** = you need **several** of these, not one.

### THE ACTION LIST — what HE needs to do to get eligible

The goal of every item below is the same: **earn more substantial, independent
articles specifically about him.** Ranked by impact.

**1. Get ONE or TWO in-depth PROFILE / FEATURE articles about him (highest impact).**
   Not event coverage — a piece whose subject *is Olamide Sax*: his story, his
   practice, an interview. Targets, in rough order of Wikipedia value:

     an event mention). *How:* pitch music editors / arts desks; work with a PR
     person or the venues' press contacts; use a strong performance or the award
     as the news hook.
   - **Nigerian national press** doing a proper profile (The Guardian NG has
     already reviewed him — a follow-up *interview/profile* would be ideal).
   - **Established Afrobeat / world-music blogs and magazines** with editorial
     teams (e.g. genre magazines, not personal blogs).
   > Practical: he/his team should prepare a one-page press kit (the EPK page +
   > the downloadable pack already do this) and pitch it to arts & music
   > journalists with a clear hook ("Eko Heritage Best Instrumentalist 2026",

**2. Turn the BBC mention into stronger coverage.**
   The current BBC piece mentions him within event coverage. *How to level up:*
   pitch a BBC regional arts producer (BBC Yorkshire / BBC Radio Sheffield /
   BBC Introducing) for an **interview or featured segment about him** — that
   becomes a citation *about him*, which is what counts. Being *in* a BBC article
   isn't the same as being *the subject* of one.

**3. Keep every new performance producing press.**
   Each festival / theatre run / award is a chance for coverage. Ask promoters
   and venues to secure press, and keep a running list of every article with its
   URL and date. (The site's press page is already this list — keep feeding it.)

**4. Get listed in music databases (parallel track, lower bar).**
   MusicBrainz + Wikidata (see Part A). These don't make him Wikipedia-notable
   by themselves, but they build his web entity and are the kind of corroboration
   reviewers like to see.

**5. When there are 2–3+ solid independent profiles, submit via Articles for
   Creation.**
   A **neutral third party** (not Olamide, not his manager — conflict-of-interest
   rules) creates a Wikipedia account and submits the draft in
   `wikipedia-draft.md` through
   [Articles for Creation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation).
   An experienced Wikipedia editor then reviews it. If it's declined, the reviewer
   says what's missing — usually "needs more independent coverage" — which loops
   back to item 1.

### What NOT to do (these get pages deleted / him blocked)

- ❌ **Don't write it himself or have his team write/submit it** — conflict of
  interest is a fast track to deletion.
- ❌ **Don't pay a "we'll put you on Wikipedia" service** — these use fake
  references and the pages get deleted; some are scams.
- ❌ **Don't cite his own website, social media, or the orgs he works with** as
  proof of notability — reviewers discount all of these.
- ❌ **Don't create it before the coverage exists** — a rejected page makes the
  next attempt harder.

### Realistic timeline

With active PR effort, artists in his position usually reach the Wikipedia bar
over **~6–18 months** of continued coverage. The two Guardian reviews mean he's
already partway. **The fastest lever is one strong national profile/interview
article.** Get one or two of those and a page becomes very submittable.
